The bundled NotSoFatso is the FamiStudio Extended modified version.
Changes made in October 2026 include NSF2 timer IRQ execution and diagnostics,
MMC5 PCM output, and register-timing validation support.
The original GNU GPL version 2-or-later notices remain applicable.
Complete corresponding source and build projects are distributed alongside
the Windows binary as FamiStudio-Extended-r12-source.zip, under
ThirdParty/NotSoFatso/, with the same source revision in the release manifest.
